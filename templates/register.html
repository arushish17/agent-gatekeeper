Prototype Policy

Why Explicit Registration is Mandatory

Only registered agents can access permitted banking actions. Unknown agents are blocked under Rule 1.

Preventing Zombie Access

Registered agents retain a permanent record. If a customer no longer wants an agent to have access, the Revoke Agent interface can revoke it.

Customer Authorisation and Audit Trail

Bill payments up to ₹10,000 are allowed only when explicitly pre-authorised. Other bill payments and all money transfers require customer approval. Registration events are recorded in audit_logs.json.
