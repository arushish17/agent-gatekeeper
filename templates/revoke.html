The Wells Fargo Precedent

American Banker Case

Why Banks Demand Proof of Revocation

In 2024, reporting revealed that Wells Fargo enacted strict policies regarding AI financial aggregators and delegated bots. When a customer signs up for an AI tool that reads balances or makes payments, the bank remains liable for fraudulent transfers under Electronic Fund Transfer Act (Regulation E) unless it can demonstrate that an authorized session was compromised or the customer failed to revoke access.

To resolve disputes, Wells Fargo requires customers to prove they explicitly revoked access. Agent Gatekeeper solves this by creating an immutable, timestamped record in agents.json and logging the revocation event in audit_logs.json.
